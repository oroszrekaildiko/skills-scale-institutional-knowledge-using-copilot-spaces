# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads define and execute the quality strategy, manage test planning and execution, and validate acceptance criteria. They partner with the product and delivery teams to ensure features meet quality standards before release.

### Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain test strategies and test plans for features
- Define acceptance criteria validation approach with Product Manager and team
- Execute and coordinate testing (unit, integration, end-to-end, performance)
- Triage and document defects with clear reproduction steps
- Participate in release readiness reviews
- Advocate for quality standards and technical debt reduction

### Goals
- Ensure all features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards
- Reduce production defects and improve reliability
- Accelerate feedback loops through efficient test automation

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Works with **Developers** to define testable acceptance criteria and review test strategies
- Partners with **Product Managers** to validate feature acceptance before release
- Collaborates with **Project Managers** on release readiness and quality metrics
- Provides quality input to **Technical Leads** on performance and system-level testing

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and retrospectives
- Acceptance criteria reviews with product and development teams
- Defect triage and closure discussions
- Release readiness signoffs with Project Manager and stakeholders

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide technical direction, conduct design reviews, and make system-level decisions. They mentor developers, identify technical risks, and ensure the solution aligns with long-term architecture and scalability goals.

### Responsibilities
- Review and approve technical designs for major features
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigation strategies
- Mentor developers and lead technical discussions
- Ensure adherence to coding standards, security, and performance guidelines
- Contribute to or approve significant architectural changes
- Partner with infrastructure and platform teams on dependencies

### Goals
- Maintain code quality, maintainability, and scalability
- Reduce technical debt and unplanned rework
- Accelerate development by removing technical blockers

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Guides **Developers** through design reviews and mentorship
- Advises **Project Managers** on technical risks and feasibility during planning
- Collaborates with **QA/Testing Leads** on performance and integration testing strategies
- Partners with **Scrum Masters** to identify and resolve technical impediments
- Communicates architectural constraints and trade-offs to **Sponsors** and stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code review feedback and technical standards documentation
- Risk escalations and dependency coordination
- Technical retrospectives and learning sessions

---

## Scrum Master / Delivery Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate team processes, remove impediments, and coach the team on agile practices. They ensure the team stays focused on goals and maintains sustainable delivery pace.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, planning, reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and help resolve team blockers and process impediments
- Coach team members on agile practices and continuous improvement
- Protect the team's focus and minimize context-switching
- Escalate cross-team dependencies and external blockers
- Track and optimize team velocity and cycle time

### Goals
- Maximize team productivity and sustainability
- Create psychological safety and continuous improvement culture
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Supports **Developers** by removing blockers and facilitating collaboration
- Works with **Project Managers** to align on schedules and escalation paths
- Coaches **QA/Testing Leads** and team members on process improvements
- Facilitates communication between **Technical Leads** and development teams
- Escalates organizational blockers to **Sponsors** and stakeholders through Project Manager

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and ceremony facilitation
- One-on-ones with team members to understand blockers
- Escalation summaries to PM and Product Lead
- Team health retrospectives and continuous improvement sessions

---

## Sponsor / Executive Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Sponsors provide strategic direction, secure resources, and serve as decision-makers for business-critical trade-offs. They ensure projects align with organizational strategy and remove organizational barriers.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project charter and confirm business case
- Allocate resources and resolve resource conflicts across projects
- Make high-level trade-off decisions (scope, time, quality, cost)
- Escalate external dependencies and organizational risks
- Communicate project status and outcomes to senior leadership
- Remove organizational barriers and unblock cross-team dependencies

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers measurable business value
- Align project with organizational strategy and priorities
- Enable rapid decision-making and risk escalation

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Reviews project charter and success metrics with **Product Managers**
- Makes resource and trade-off decisions impacting **Project Managers** and delivery teams
- Receives escalations from **Project Managers** on organizational blockers
- Approves high-level architectural decisions recommended by **Technical Leads**
- Communicates outcomes to stakeholders and executives based on **Product Manager** metrics

### Typical Communication
- Project charter reviews and approvals
- Monthly executive status updates
- High-level risk and dependency escalations from Project Manager
- Go/no-go decision gates and resource allocation meetings

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge the gap between business needs and technical solutions. They gather and document requirements, create user stories, and ensure shared understanding of what needs to be built and why.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct stakeholder interviews and gather business requirements
- Document requirements and acceptance criteria in user stories
- Validate that requirements are clear, testable, and actionable
- Partner with Product Manager to translate business needs into product backlog items
- Clarify ambiguities and resolve conflicting requirements
- Document domain knowledge and business rules

### Goals
- Ensure clear, shared understanding of requirements across the team
- Reduce rework due to misunderstood or incomplete requirements
- Accelerate feature delivery by improving requirement clarity

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Partners with **Product Managers** to translate business needs into actionable backlog items
- Works with **Developers** to ensure requirements are technically feasible and clearly understood
- Collaborates with **QA/Testing Leads** to define testable acceptance criteria
- Supports **Project Managers** with requirement clarity and scope documentation
- Gathers stakeholder input for **Sponsors** and business leaders

### Typical Communication
- Requirements gathering meetings and workshops with stakeholders
- User story reviews and backlog refinement with product and development teams
- Domain expertise documentation and wikis
- Acceptance criteria validation with QA and developers
- Requirement clarification discussions during sprint planning
